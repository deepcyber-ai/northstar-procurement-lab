Quick walkthrough

  1. Open the main Northstar application in another tab. Select Sign in and use Alice's demo account.
  2. Open an order and inspect its details and workflow history.
  3. Sign out, then select Return to sign in to finish clearing the identity session.
  4. Sign in as Bob to compare the other organisation, or Ada to view administrator functionality.

Use the accounts above only in the Northstar lab. Never give the owner-page password to a scanner, paste it into the app, or use it as a demo-user password.

Access and sign-out

The owner page requires its own password plus an allowed network IP. App administrator accounts do not grant access here. Use a private browser window and close it after use: browsers may remember HTTP Basic credentials, and app sign-out does not clear them.

Synthetic data only. The sample application is deliberately vulnerable; keep its network restrictions in place.
